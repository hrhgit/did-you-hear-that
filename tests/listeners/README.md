# tests/listeners

Simulated listeners used by the property tests. A listener is a function from "a sound at this level on this side" to a response, with a chosen threshold for each ear. Any randomness in a listener comes from a seeded generator, so a run can be replayed.

- **Listener types.** `listener-types.ts` defines what a listener sees: the kind of trial, and for a sound its ear and level. `psychometric-listener.ts` is the imperfect listener, with a true threshold per ear, a psychometric width, and lapse, false alarm and wrong-side rates. `extreme-listeners.ts` holds the listeners that behave badly on purpose: always answers, never answers, answers at random, always names the wrong side, always presses left. `listener-parameter-arbitraries.ts` holds the Plausible and Attentive parameter ranges.
- **The harness.** `search-under-test.ts` is the interface every version of the search is run through, so the same checks work on any version. `spec-search-under-test.ts` adapts this repository's engine to it, and `searches-under-test.ts` lists the searches the scripts can name. `classify-accuracy.ts` says whether an ear result is Accurate (within one step of the level grid around the true threshold), Flagged or Wrong, and `is-within-five-db.ts` keeps the first, 5 dB version of that measure for comparison. `measure-population.ts` and `play-population-rooms.ts` run one room for each listener of a population drawn from one seed.
- **The scatter script.** `write-scatter-data.ts` runs `npm run experiment:scatter` and writes `docs/experiments/threshold-search-scatter-<search>.csv`, true threshold against estimate for 3000 listeners. It is not a test file, so Vitest does not collect it.
- **The simulated visitor.** `simulated-visitor.ts` plays one whole visit through the visit flow with any of the listeners above (`playVisit`). It follows a visitor script: it chooses the visit, starts the hiss and presses louder until the played setting reaches its target, keeps it, plays and answers the headphone check, and goes on from the rules, the rests between rooms and the replay after a rest. It hears of each trial only from the flow's test hook `onTrialPresented`, never from the screen, asks its listener, and answers after a delay drawn from its seeded source, with a time stamp that may be the time now, earlier, missing, not a number or in the future. It also gives the script's extra presses, its pauses and stop reports followed by a resume, and its ending, the stop control or a left page. It moves time by events, to whichever comes first of its next planned step and the timer's next callback, so that every answer lands at the planned moment. The clocks are moved by a driver, which `tests/properties/visit-flow-harness.ts` provides. A script may also give a replay behaviour for the missed-sound replay: watch it to the end, skip or stop it at a time after the start press, or follow the starts and events of a replay script. Without one the visitor goes on from the replay after a rest, which skips it. `simulated-visitor.test.ts` plays one quick visit with a step listener and checks that every caught trial is a sound at or above the listener's threshold.
- **Worn headphones.** `worn-headphones.ts` (`wornHeadphones`) puts a listener in headphones: a sound on an earpiece reaches the ear that earpiece sits on, made quieter by that earpiece's share of the imbalance H (H/2 louder on the left earpiece, H/2 quieter on the right), and a no-sound trial reaches the listener unchanged. Which earpiece sits on which ear is read at every call, so the wearer can swap the headphones and turn them back.
- **The simulated visitor in the swapped repeat.** The simulated visitor hears every trial through its own Worn_Headphones, with the earpiece the flow's test hook gives (`FlowPresentedTrial`) and the arrangement it wears; it puts them on as marked, and a script may give an imbalance (`headphones`). A script may also give a swapped-repeat behaviour (`swappedRepeat`): which floor to name at the result, whether it swaps on the first instruction or confirms without swapping, its first answer to the swap check (after that it answers with the side the hiss reached in what it wears), its press delays, its events in the stage, and whether it turns the headphones back on reading the line that asks it to. Without a behaviour it stops at the result, and with an imbalance of 0 every visit plays exactly as before.
- **The measurement of the visit-level rule.** `visit-rule-measurement.ts` (`measureVisitFalseAlarmRule`) plays whole quick and full visits room by room through the shipped engine with psychometric listeners and fixed seeds, judges every ear with `classifyAccuracy`, and applies the session's `judgeVisitFalseAlarms` at each candidate limit. `print-visit-rule-measurement.ts` runs it for the cases of the visit-flow design and prints the tables as Markdown. It writes no file and has no `package.json` script:

  ```
  npx tsx tests/listeners/print-visit-rule-measurement.ts
  ```

  The tables are what the project owner chooses the visit limit from. The limit in config, 0.2, was decided by the owner's coordinator on 2026-11-16, at the owner's request; the owner may reverse it (Q1 in `.kiro/specs/visit-flow/design.md`). Neither file is a test file, so Vitest does not collect them. The statistical property CP11 calls the same function.
- **The measurement of the replay's length.** `replay-length-measurement.ts` (`measureReplayLength`) plays rooms through the shipped engine with `playPopulationRooms`, takes them as many at a time as a visit has rooms, and counts each visit's missed sounds and answers on the wrong side. For each most count (20, 30, 45, 60 and no limit) and each gap (300, 400 and 600 ms) it gives how often the replay is a selection and how long it lasts. The most counts and gaps are parameters of the measurement; it changes no config value and chooses nothing. `print-replay-length-measurement.ts` runs it with the ranges, seeds and visit counts of "Measurements" in `.kiro/specs/missed-sound-replay/design.md` and prints the tables as Markdown. It writes no file and has no `package.json` script:

  ```
  npx tsx tests/listeners/print-replay-length-measurement.ts
  ```

  The tables are what the project owner chooses the most count and the gap from (Q1 and Q2 of that design). Their figures come from the engine alone, with one listener per room, and say nothing about real visitors. Neither file is a test file, so Vitest does not collect them.
- **The measurement of the attribution.** `attribution-measurement.ts` (`measureAttribution`) plays each pair of an ear difference and an imbalance on a grid from −40 to 40 dB through the shipped engine twice, once as marked and once swapped, with Step_Listeners and with Attentive and Plausible listeners in Worn_Headphones, and counts the answers against the brief's sentences read as written and against the rule applied to the true differences, with one and two steps of tolerance, and the answers where one of the two is zero. It chooses nothing and changes no config value. `print-attribution-measurement.ts` runs it with the seeds and run counts of "Measurements" in `.kiro/specs/swapped-repeat/design.md` and prints the tables as Markdown, with the method. It writes no file and has no `package.json` script:

  ```
  npx tsx tests/listeners/print-attribution-measurement.ts
  ```

  The tables bear on how "Correct attribution" is stated (Q1, decided by the owner on 2026-11-15) and on which listeners it uses (Q2, decided by the owner's coordinator on 2026-11-16, at the owner's request; the owner may reverse it). Their figures come from the engine alone and say nothing about real visitors. Neither file is a test file, so Vitest does not collect them.
