# src/audio/output

The only code that touches Web Audio. Every sound the product plays goes through this module (audio-safety rule 1). It renders each sound itself from a Sound_Id, applies the output fade, the cap and the rise limit, routes the sound to one ear with exact zeros on the other, and stops all sound on request or when the page is hidden or left. Sound-producing browser APIs are allowed here and nowhere else in `src/`. The design is `.kiro/specs/audio-output/design.md`.

May import from: `config`, `audio/synth`.

## The one entry point

`createAudioOutput({ backend, timer })` in `create-audio-output.ts` returns the output shell. Code outside this module plays sound only through it: `activate` from inside a click or key press, `beginCalibration`, `playCalibrationRepeat` (the hiss, the calibration sound where the music cannot be loaded) and `keepCalibration` for the calibration, `playHeadphoneCheck`, `beginRoom` and `play` for the rooms, and `stopAll` for the stop control. For the calibration music it also has `calibrationSound`, `onCalibrationSoundChange`, `calibrationControlTopDb`, `startCalibrationMusic`, `setCalibrationMusicTarget`, `calibrationMusicSettingDb`, `onCalibrationMusicStep` and `stopCalibrationMusic` (`CalibrationMusicOutput`). A caller cannot hand it samples. Every outcome says the level asked for, the level played and the limit that lowered it, if any, and `clampLog` keeps every sound that was played lower than asked.

## Layers

- **The pure core.** `prepare-playback.ts` turns a request (samples, sample rate, ear, requested level, calibration gain, previous gains) into the two channels that will be played, or a refusal. It applies the output fade (`apply-output-fade.ts`), the anchor cap, the rise limit and the absolute ceiling, in that order, and never throws. `render-playable-buffer.ts` renders a sound and renders it again while its peak is above its bound. `room-top-level.ts` and `range-report.ts` give the highest level each room can be played at for a calibration. `calibration-setting-top.ts` and `next-calibration-setting.ts` define the calibration control, and `measure-calibration-music.ts`, `calibration-music-top.ts` and `next-calibration-music-setting.ts` measure and step the calibration music. `gain-to-decibels.ts` converts a gain to decibels. All of these run under Node.
- **The shell.** `create-audio-output.ts` holds what the core cannot: whether sound may play, the kept calibration, the previous gain on each ear, one sound at a time, the clamp log, and every stop. It reaches the browser only through the `AudioBackend` interface in `audio-backend.ts`, which names no Web Audio type. `output-types.ts` holds the shared types.
- **The backends.** `web-audio-backend.ts` and `output-graph.ts` are the only files that name Web Audio types. The backend creates the `AudioContext` at the device's own rate and turns page events into stops. The graph plays the two channels as they are through one stop gain, whose 20 ms fade brings every channel to exact zero on a stop. `channel-index.ts` is the one place where `left` and `right` become channel 0 and 1. `fake-audio-backend.ts` records every call for the tests under Node. Production code never imports it.

## The calibration music

The calibration music (`.kiro/specs/audible-start/design.md`, "The calibration music") is the one sound the shell plays that has no Sound_Id, and the one that loops: the exception to audio-safety rule 6, approved by the project owner on 2026-11-16.

- **Loading.** The first `activate` that creates the context asks the backend for `calibrationMusicConfig.filePath` once for the life of the shell. The backend fetches it from the site's own files, decodes it with the context and gives the first channel at the context's rate. The shell measures it with `measure-calibration-music.ts` and works out the top of the control with `calibration-music-top.ts`. The Calibration_Sound is `loading` until then, `music` when the samples are usable, and `hiss` for the rest of the shell's life when the fetch or the decoding fails, the samples are empty, silent or not finite, the top would leave fewer than `calibrationSettingSpanMinDb` above the Keep_Minimum, or nothing has arrived after `calibrationMusicLoadMaxMs`. A result after the time-out is dropped.
- **Playing.** `startCalibrationMusic` loops the samples in both channels from the bottom of the control, fading in from exact zero over the output fade. Every `calibrationMusicGainStepMs` the injected timer moves the level one step of `next-calibration-music-setting.ts` towards the target, at most 0.25 dB, as an exponential ramp that never begins before the last one has ended on the audio clock, so it rises by at most 5 dB in 2 seconds. The level gain is never above the ceiling over the measured peak. While the music plays every other sound is refused `busy`. `stopCalibrationMusic`, a kept calibration and every stop fade it to exact zero. Keeping with the music keeps the setting it reached, rounded down to the grid.
- **In the graph.** The loop's source goes through its level gain, its fade-in gain and its fade-out gain into the stop gain, so `fadeToSilence` stops it with every other sound. No automation on its path is cancelled.

## Where it is checked

Unit tests sit next to each file. The properties CP1 to CP13 are in `tests/properties/audio-output-cp*.property.test.ts`, and the music's, audible-start CP6, in `tests/properties/audible-start-cp6-music-is-safe.property.test.ts`. `tests/e2e/listening-page.spec.ts` checks the Web Audio files in headless Chromium.
